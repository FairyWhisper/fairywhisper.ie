FairyWhisper is neither a Fairy, nor a whisperer, she's just an Amanda, existing up Fairy Hill, doing fairy things.

very mysterious, really.

Wrangling wild children, animals that seem to turn up at her doorstep, and attempting to keep the shack from falling down around them all.

Not very successfully

Sometimes it's worth remembering that there's life beyond the button, though. Hard, generally confusing, sometimes bizarre, but always there, always available for whoever needs a place, a hug, or the enormous sense of wellbeing that comes through comparison to the ordinary.

Former Lives

Amanda has been a jack of literally all trades, anda master of science (virology). There was some retail, some work in the charity sector, some database admin and a brief foray into Vet School that was scuppered by banks not giving college loans to "the poors".

Current Life

Now in her final evolved form, she's a fairy that lives in a shack up a hill in rural Roscommon with the wildlings.

Future Plans

BookKeeper

For anyone who keeps a home library, or reads a lot. There are catalogue and library applications already, sure, so why is FairyWhisper making this one? Simple. Because a physical book tells at least two stories.

Listing your possessions accurately is great and all. Shelving them properly and having access to what you want when you want it, also amazing. But there's a story in every book you've kept that doesn't lie between the covers.

If you have immediately thought of a book you own while reading this, BookKeeper is for you.

With BookKeeper, you can document not just the bibliographic details of your books, but also the personal stories, memories, and experiences associated with each one. It's a way to preserve the narrative of your home library beyond the physical shelves.

BookKeeper is designed to be cross-platform, ensuring that your library's story is accessible wherever you are, whether on your desktop, tablet, or mobile device. It's more than just an app; it's a companion for your literary journey.

But of course, there's also Dewey Classification, Shelf-Finder, records for physical condition, legacy management and all the things that make a comprehensive home library management system truly effective.

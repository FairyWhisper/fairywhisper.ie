A Little Whisper

October 10, 2026

In the beginning ...

There was an Amanda. A dub, livng the shack-life, up a hill in rural Roscommon. Some know her as FairyWhisper. Some know her as words I won't publish here. And two know her as "mam."

So I guess I can't really start at the very beginning, my apologies to Julie Andrews. Because our story, that of FairyWhisper, of WildFairy (7F) and of WildWhisper (6M) is the present, not the past.

All good stories start at a "time of great change" though, don't they. And hopefully, this is one such time. I, AC, FairyWhisper, have gone back to Uni, to learn a completely new, and completely terrifying new field of study. Software Development, here I come

WildFairy and WildWhisper are settled in their lovely school, both in full days now. The shack is mostly prit-sticked together, the worst bits hanging on with thoughts prayers and a lot of DIY learning. The time is now. So, it may not be the beginning. But it is a beginning.

I've never written a blog before, so please, bear with me as I bambi-step my way through this whole shebang! The general plan is to weave all the pieces of thread I've half-sewn together, into a coherent tapestry of our lives, our adventures, and our little whispers of magic.

I'll work on being less cheesey for the next post. I Promise!

For now, feel free to have a rummage around the workshop that is FairyWhisper.ie!
